EdgeVLM-Drive · Master’s graduation project

A compact vision-language model that recognizes four driver states (normal, drowsy, looking away, phone use) offline on a 4 GB Jetson Nano. I built the driver-split data pipeline, corrected fragmented drowsy labels (2.5× more correct frames), and verified external data with a MediaPipe eye-aspect-ratio check. I also contributed to LoRA fine-tuning of SmolVLM-500M and deploying SmolVLM-256M.

EV Charging Energy Prediction · IEEE ITC-Egypt 2026

An interpretable soft sensor that predicts total session energy at plug-in time from connection metadata only. A 500-tree Random Forest reached RMSE 3.11 kWh on 12,839 real sessions, beating Linear Regression by 32%. User behaviour drives 65.8% of the signal, which supports proactive grid planning.
